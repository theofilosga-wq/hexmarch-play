Hexmarch map preview: today's ground next to the new "bands" ground

Each pair below is the same board twice: the same armies, the same rocks, forests, hills and shrines; on the left the coloured ground is laid out the way the game does it today, on the right the way the new "bands" idea would do it, where every army gets the same shapes turned to its own seat, with a home patch by its start, a frontier patch on the way to the middle, and a few far outposts near the Heart or on a neighbour's flank.

Today about 72 percent of the ground is coloured; the new layout aims for about 55 percent, so more of the board is neutral and can be claimed during the game, and the small print above each map says how much is coloured and whether every army keeps an open route to the Heart and owns a tile near it.

What to judge: do the right-hand maps look fairer and more interesting to fight over than the left-hand ones, is the amount of colour right, and are the far outposts in places you would actually want to reach? Nothing has changed in the game yet; this page only shows examples.
