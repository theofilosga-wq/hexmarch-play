    Armies in detail

    Tap a column to sort. First out is how often the army was the first one eliminated. Plain dmg is the share of the army's damage dealt by units with no ability; pawn dmg by pawns alone. AP left is unspent action points per turn.

    Points by source

    Warlord points each army scored per game, split by where they came from: holding the Heart, holding an Outpost, and Leader takedowns (2 points each). The line under the table shows where the winners' points came from.

    Every unit

    Averages per unit per game. Dmg / HP is damage dealt divided by the unit's max HP, a rough measure of value for its size. Idle is how often the unit never attacked or used an ability all game.

    Every ability and Ultimate

    Per game the army played. Per use is damage each time the ability is cast. Basic attacks, curse ticks, burning ground, strike-backs and death blasts are listed too, so you can see where each army's damage really comes from.
